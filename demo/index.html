k-mix-api 2.0 demo

Interactive K-Mix control surface — drag faders & rotaries, click buttons. Powered by k-mix-api (on midi-ports). SVG adapted from k-mix-api-mixer-demo.

idle

No K-Mix attached? The surface still works — dragging a control sends a MIDI message (it just warns until the ports appear). With K-Mix in a MIDI Bank mode, moving a physical control drives the on-screen one.
